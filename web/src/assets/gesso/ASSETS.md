## Landing showcase reel (2026-10-22)

`/media/gesso-landing-reel.mp4` now cuts five real motion renders from the user-supplied `gesso-video-handoff/media/03-renders/loops/`: Omega Speedmaster fan art, Ambre perfume, Leica look-alike, Theo teapot and Sasha ring. These are the motion counterparts of the current Form + light stills, not newly rendered films or Minolta footage. Each source is 1280 × 720 / 30 fps / 6.70 s, rendered by Gesso in Cycles at 32 samples with Metal and OIDN (handoff `repo-extras/final-campaigns.txt`). The cut keeps frames 134–199 inclusive of each source, in that order: five 2.2 s push-ins, 11 s total, clean cuts, no synthetic camera motion. Audio is removed. The tracked H.264 file is about 1.1 MB; the original loops and media outside the repository remain untouched.

`landing-reel.json` is the shared source-range and playback-timing manifest. Rebuild with `scripts/build_landing_reel.py --source-dir <handoff>/media/03-renders/loops --ffmpeg <ffmpeg>`. `landing-showcase-poster.webp` is the exact encoded opening frame. The latest 64-sample `form-light-renders/omega-speedmaster.webp` supplies the reduced-motion, data-saving and failed-video still. Labels and credits come from `PRODUCT_GALLERY` in `productDescription.ts`; the camera is the Leica look-alike by Lamashtu, not the Minolta XD7. All per-model credits and source reuse limits remain in force; no new licence is asserted. Worlds are the user's World Labs generations and authored colour-field studios.

At the user's request, the homepage film/stills and About gallery video/posters are mirrored horizontally in CSS. The source media and the encoded reel keep their original orientation. Text, controls and other page images are not mirrored. A video edit using the source reel should apply the manifest's `flip: true` once to match the site; a recording of the site is already mirrored.

## Form + Light interface compositions (2026-10-21)

`editor-stills/*.webp` are exports of the five editable compositions in the [gesso Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/yeSSVUrYYw45JLkN7mnv52/gesso?node-id=342-8486), section “09 / Form + light — interface compositions”. They arrange Gesso's controls and existing product/material previews for each aperture; they are composed illustrations of the interface, not unaltered screenshots or final Cycles output. Halyard text, icons, fields and instances remain editable in Figma.

`form-light-renders/*.webp` replace the editor stills in Form + light (D161). Each is a single Cycles frame rendered by Gesso on 2026-10-21 from the showcase campaigns (`tools/render/matrix.json` in the video project), at the aperture's own aspect and 64 samples, then encoded with `cwebp -q 90 -sharp_yuv`: Omega Speedmaster fan art in the velvet jewel-box (frame 60, 1512 × 1512; ArakanI, Blendswap, CC0), Sasha ring in the flooded marble hall (frame 40, 1512 × 1512; saber7711, Blendswap, CC BY), Ambre perfume bottle in the brutalist atrium (frame 30, 1220 × 1632; Esteban Paris Ambre bottle via Blendswap), Leica look-alike in the blue field (frame 40, 1512 × 994; Lamashtu, Blendswap, CC0) and Stelton Theo teapot set in the orange field (frame 185, 1512 × 1066; Blendswap, CC0). Worlds are the user's World Labs generations. Same credits as the About gallery.

Each content frame was exported to PNG at 1.5× with absolute bounds, excluding its enclosing mask and review-board labels, then encoded as lossless WebP. Decoded RGBA pixels match the PNG exports exactly. The website centers each rectangle and applies its own moving aperture once. Stage 0 retains the live glassine shader.
